Pit-lane capability

Built for riders chasing race-ready speed.

PTR supports road, race, drag, speedway, motocross, and offroad motorcycles with mechanical development that is practical, measured, and proven on track.

01

Dyno tuning

Dynojet 250ix Eddy Current dyno tuning with high speed ram air simulation, closed-loop tuning, Woolich Racing Software, Power Commander, ECU tuning, and other tuning devices.

02

Cylinder head

Serdi valve seat cutting, Rottler machinery, and SuperFlow 600 flow bench work to measure, graph, port, and develop cylinder heads for drag, circuit, and motocross power.

03

Suspension

Computerised Roehrig suspension dyno work to measure front forks and rear shocks before modifying and revalving to suit exact rider and bike requirements.

04

Chassis

Laser aligning equipment, digital scales, inclinometers, steering head angle checks, wheel alignment, and balance data entered into ZERO CHASSIS software for fast, confidence-inspiring setup.

05

Machine shop

First-class in-house machine shop capability for intricate engine and suspension machining, preparation, and modification work.

06

Gearboxes & crankshafts

Gearbox drive dog undercutting, isotropic surface finishing, gear shaft work, and specialised modifications for smoother shifts, reduced friction, longevity, drag racing, and road racing.
